Decision support

Distinguish a credible procurement route from an expensive distraction

Iraqi opportunities can be public tenders, limited invitations, investment proposals, direct private procurements, subcontract packages or early announcements. Each requires a different evidence threshold. The first task is to understand who is buying, what stage exists and what the bidder must prove.

Is the opportunity authentic?

Check the issuing entity, document source, reference number, procurement route, deadline and available official publication or confirmation.

Is it actually live?

Separate concepts, budget proposals, investment listings, prequalification, active tenders, awards and projects under implementation.

Can the company qualify?

Review stated experience, registration, licences, turnover, guarantees, tax, social-security, local presence and other eligibility conditions.

What must be local?

Identify Arabic documentation, local registrations, agents, subcontractors, site visits, permits, pricing inputs and submission logistics.

Is the commercial risk acceptable?

Flag payment mechanism, currency, security, bid and performance guarantees, delay exposure, taxes, import duties and contract-law questions.

Who owns each action?

Create a controlled responsibility matrix across the client, local partners, lawyers, banks, insurers, technical advisers and document team.

Common questions

Iraq tender and bid-support questions

Can you find Iraqi tenders for our sector?

Tender monitoring can be scoped for defined sectors and issuing entities, but listings must be verified against the primary notice and current tender documents. A public opportunity list is not treated as proof that a procurement remains open.

Can a foreign company bid without an Iraqi branch?

Eligibility depends on the tender and contracting entity. Some procurements permit international bidders while others require a registered branch, local documentation or an Iraqi partner. The exact documents and applicable rules must be checked for each opportunity.

Do you prepare the technical and financial bid?

Middle East Nexus can coordinate the commercial and local workstream. The client and its appointed technical, legal and financial advisers remain responsible for specialist content, pricing, certifications and final approval unless separately contracted.

Can you confirm that a project has budget?

We seek documentary or direct evidence about the funding and procurement route, but budget references can change and do not guarantee payment. Contract-specific payment security and public-finance questions require detailed review.

What should we send for an initial review?

Provide the complete tender or opportunity document, source link, correspondence, deadline, your proposed role, company credentials, intended partners and the key concern driving the review.
