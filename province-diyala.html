Regional Market Profile

Baqubah & Diyala

Diyala lies east of Baghdad and connects the capital region with Iran. Its economy is closely linked to agriculture, orchards, food production, cross-border trade, construction and public infrastructure. Water management and reliable logistics are central to investment planning.

Published as a strategic market-entry overview. Individual tenders, land status, licences, financing and procurement routes must be confirmed directly with the responsible Iraqi authority.
