The Project

The Largest Water Treatment Project Ever Built in Iraq.

The Nasiriyah Water Treatment Plant is a landmark of Iraqi reconstruction — a $277 million facility producing 10,000 cubic metres of potable water every hour. It supplies five cities across Dhi Qar province and serves 1.47 million people daily, built with 118 kilometres of transmission pipeline and 48,000 cubic metres of concrete.

For a project of this magnitude in southern Iraq, the hardest part was never the engineering — it was the supply chain. Getting construction materials to site, in the required volume, on schedule, through customs and across a challenging security environment, is where most projects stall.

Our Role

Middle East Nexus supplied cement and construction materials directly to this project — part of the operation that delivered up to 1,000 tonnes of cement per day to Iraq's water infrastructure at peak, alongside customs clearance and logistics across southern Iraq. This is what execution at scale looks like in practice.

This record is why international companies work with us. Not a slide deck — a supply chain that has already delivered on one of the largest infrastructure projects Iraq has undertaken.
