Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about petroleum product trading and documentation.

How do I source petroleum products like Gas Oil or Mazut from Iraq?

Sourcing typically begins with a verified seller or allocation holder, agreed product specifications confirmed by an independent lab report, and a clear procedure covering price, delivery terms and inspection. Working with a party who understands both the documentation and the on-the-ground realities in Iraq and Kurdistan reduces the risk of unreliable counterparties.

What is an LOI in petroleum trading?

An LOI (Letter of Intent) is a document issued by the buyer expressing genuine interest in purchasing a product. It is not legally binding and should state the product, quantity, specification and desired terms — but it should never contain the buyer's banking or financial details.

What is an ICPO and how is it different from an LOI?

An ICPO (Irrevocable Corporate Purchase Order) is a more formal, semi-binding commitment issued by the buyer after the LOI. Unlike the LOI, it usually includes the buyer's banking details and transaction procedure, and sellers rely on it to confirm the buyer is serious before issuing a Full Corporate Offer or contract.

Why does fuel quality and lab testing matter?

Product quality is confirmed through an independent lab report (for example SGS) against agreed specifications. For products like Gas Oil, Diesel and HFO/Mazut, verifying quality before commitment protects the buyer from off-spec cargo and pricing disputes, and is a standard part of any serious transaction.
