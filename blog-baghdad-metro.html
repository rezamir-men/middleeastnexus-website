Baghdad Metro, Al-Faw Port and the New Iraq: A $150 Billion Pipeline

November 2024 · Reza Mir, Founder

Iraq's National Investment Commission is overseeing one of the most ambitious infrastructure pipelines in the Middle East. With $150 billion of projects targeted across agriculture, transportation, telecommunications, energy, entertainment and housing, the scale of opportunity is extraordinary.

Flagship Projects

Baghdad Metro (DBOMFT): An international consortium has been selected to negotiate a design, build, finance, operate, maintain and transfer contract for the Baghdad metro — one of the largest public transport investments in the region's history.

Najaf-Karbala Metro: A separate metro line linking the holy cities of Najaf and Karbala, announced as Investment Opportunity No. 2 of 2024.

Al-Faw Grand Port: Being developed with UAE expertise, this deepwater port in Basra is designed to position Iraq as the region's premier trade gateway — the southern terminus of the Development Road.

Bismayah New City: A major housing development in Baghdad representing one of the largest residential projects in the Arab world.

The Construction Materials Implication

Projects of this scale consume extraordinary volumes of cement, steel, glass, aggregates and specialist construction materials. For suppliers positioned with the right local partner, Iraq's $150 billion pipeline represents a multi-decade commercial opportunity.
