Decision support

Answer the questions that determine whether entry is commercially viable

Iraq is not one uniform market. Federal Iraq and the Kurdistan Region have different institutional routes, while buyer access, project authority, logistics and local execution vary by sector and governorate. The service focuses on the decisions that must be resolved before a company spends heavily on visits, registration or representation.

Where is the real demand?

Separate broad national narratives from the buyer groups, project pipelines and locations relevant to the client's offer.

Which route to market fits?

Compare direct export, distributor, commercial agent, local company, foreign branch, project vehicle and consortium routes at a commercial level.

Who actually has authority?

Map ministries, state companies, investment commissions, governorates, private buyers and other stakeholders without assuming that visibility equals decision authority.

What must be verified first?

Identify legal, tax, banking, sanctions, registration, licensing, payment and operational questions that require specialist confirmation.

Which partner profile is needed?

Define capabilities, geography, licences, relationships, balance-sheet strength and conflicts before beginning an introduction process.

What is the first practical move?

Build a staged plan for validation, meetings, documentation, local presence and early commercial activity rather than beginning with an expensive full launch.

Common questions

Iraq market-entry consulting: practical questions

Do we need to register a company before testing the Iraqi market?

Not necessarily. The appropriate sequence depends on the activity, buyer, tender rules, contracting model and whether the company will trade or employ locally. Early market validation can often be completed before a full operating structure is chosen, but Iraqi legal and tax advice should confirm what is permitted for the intended activity.

Does one strategy cover both Federal Iraq and the Kurdistan Region?

No. A national strategy may cover both, but the institutional, registration, procurement and operating routes must be assessed separately. The right choice depends on the target customers, project location, sector and intended legal presence.

Can Middle East Nexus arrange meetings in Iraq?

Meeting support can form part of a scoped mandate after the target stakeholder list and commercial purpose are agreed. A meeting is treated as a validation step, not as proof of authority, funding or future award.

How long does an initial market-entry assessment take?

Timing depends on sector, geography and evidence requirements. A focused first assessment can be scoped around a limited decision question; broader mandates involving partner checks, visits or project verification require additional stages.

What information should we provide at the start?

A useful brief includes the product or service, target buyer, preferred geography, existing Iraq contacts, commercial objective, budget range, desired timeline and any opportunity or tender documents already received.
