Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

How this site uses storage

Cookies are small text files placed on a device. Browsers also provide similar storage technologies, including local storage. This site uses a necessary local-storage preference and, only if you accept, Google Analytics cookies.

Necessary preference storage

men_analytics_choice is stored in your browser's local storage after you accept or decline analytics. It records only the value “accepted” or “declined” so that we can respect your choice on later pages and visits. It remains until you change the choice or clear browser data. It is not used for advertising or cross-site tracking.

Optional Google Analytics

Google Analytics is disabled unless you select “Accept analytics”. When enabled, it helps us understand page views and interactions so we can improve the site's usefulness. We configure it without advertising personalisation or Google Signals.

  • _ga: distinguishes browsers for analytics; may last up to 2 years.
  • _ga_<measurement-id>: maintains session-related analytics state; may last up to 2 years.

Google may process technical information under its own terms. Analytics data is not used by us to identify you directly.

Your choice

The initial banner provides equally available accept and decline controls. After making a choice, use the “Cookie settings” control at the bottom of any page to change it. Declining or later withdrawing consent disables analytics and attempts to remove Google Analytics cookies created for this site. You can also clear cookies and local storage through your browser settings.

Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal, and declining analytics does not limit access to the site.

Other services

Following a link to another website may allow that website to use its own cookies. Submitting a form sends the information you choose to Formspree for delivery; consult the relevant provider's privacy information for its separate processing.

Contact and updates

We may update this policy if our technology or legal requirements change. Questions can be sent to info@middleeastnexus.co.uk.

Google Analytics الاختياري

تبقى Google Analytics معطّلة ما لم تختر «قبول التحليلات». وعند تفعيلها تساعدنا في فهم مشاهدات الصفحات والتفاعلات لتحسين فائدة الموقع. نضبطها من دون تخصيص إعلاني أو Google Signals.

  • _ga: يميّز المتصفحات لأغراض التحليل وقد يبقى حتى سنتين.
  • _ga_<measurement-id>: يحفظ حالة مرتبطة بجلسة التحليلات وقد يبقى حتى سنتين.

قد تعالج Google معلومات تقنية وفق شروطها، ولا نستخدم بيانات التحليلات للتعرّف عليك مباشرة.
