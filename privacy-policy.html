Who we are

Middle East Nexus Ltd is the controller of personal data described in this notice. We are registered in England and Wales under company number 15259014. Our contact email is info@middleeastnexus.co.uk.

Information we collect

When you contact us or submit a consultation or opportunity form, we may collect your name, business email, telephone number if supplied, company, country, sector, target governorate, support required, estimated timeline, message and other project or opportunity details you choose to provide.

We also record limited attribution data needed to understand and respond to the enquiry, such as the page used, referring page and campaign parameters. If you consent to analytics, Google Analytics may process device, browser, approximate location, page-view and interaction information and assign online identifiers.

How and why we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and take steps requested before a possible engagement — legitimate interests and, where applicable, steps before entering a contract.
  • To provide agreed advisory or commercial-support services — performance of a contract.
  • To protect our forms, website and communications from abuse or fraud — legitimate interests.
  • To understand website use through optional analytics — consent.
  • To meet legal, accounting and regulatory obligations — legal obligation.

We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

Service providers and sharing

We share personal data only where reasonably necessary. Providers may include Formspree for form delivery, our email provider, GitHub Pages and Cloudflare for website delivery and security, Google Fonts for typeface delivery, and Google Analytics only after analytics consent. We may also disclose information to professional advisers, authorities or courts where required by law or necessary to establish or defend legal rights.

These providers act under their own terms or data-processing arrangements. We do not share enquiry data with third parties for their independent marketing.

International transfers

Some technology providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where UK data-protection law requires safeguards, we rely on an adequacy regulation, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, the UK Addendum to approved contractual clauses, or another lawful transfer mechanism as applicable.

Retention

  • Enquiries that do not become an engagement: normally up to 24 months after the last meaningful contact.
  • Client, contract, accounting and related correspondence: normally up to 6 years after the relevant engagement ends, unless law or a dispute requires longer.
  • Form-security information: normally up to 12 months.
  • Google Analytics event-level information: up to 14 months, subject to the analytics settings and Google's own processing rules.

We may keep information for a shorter period when it is no longer necessary.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal data, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing. Use the Cookie settings control for analytics, or email us for other requests.

You may complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, although we would welcome the opportunity to address your concern first.

Security, changes and contact

We use reasonable organisational and technical measures appropriate to the information we handle. No internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

We may update this notice when our services or legal obligations change. The date above identifies the current version. Questions or rights requests should be sent to info@middleeastnexus.co.uk.

This website privacy notice is intended to describe our current website and enquiry practices. A separate engagement notice or contract may apply when we provide services to a client.
