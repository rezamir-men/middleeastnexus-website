Clear boundaries

What this service does not promise

Middle East Nexus provides commercial intelligence, coordination and practical market-access support. The following boundaries protect both the client and the quality of the engagement.

  • Customs classification, valuation, duty, permit and release decisions remain with the competent authorities and qualified customs or legal professionals.
  • Middle East Nexus coordinates with licensed brokers and carriers where required; it does not misdescribe cargo or bypass customs, sanctions or export-control rules.
  • Border status, waiting times, security and route availability are time-sensitive and must be reconfirmed immediately before dispatch.
  • Indicative routes and costs are not binding freight quotations, insurance cover or guarantees of clearance or delivery time.

Common questions

Iraq customs and logistics questions

Which is the best route for shipping goods into Iraq?

There is no single best route. The decision depends on cargo, origin, Iraqi destination, importer, permits, border status, terminal services, cost, insurance, sanctions and final-mile risk. A route should be selected against the actual shipment data.

Can you clear goods through Iraqi customs?

Middle East Nexus can coordinate the commercial and local workstream and work with appropriately licensed customs brokers where required. Formal declarations, classification and authority-facing actions remain with authorised parties.

Can Iran be used as a transit route to Iraq?

Some routes may be physically available, but sanctions, export controls, banking, insurance, carrier policy, origin rules and cargo restrictions can make them unsuitable. The full transaction and route require enhanced compliance review before commitment.

What information is needed for a route assessment?

Provide product description, assumed HS code, quantity, weight and dimensions, origin, pickup point, Iraqi destination, value, Incoterms, target date, consignee, permits and any dangerous, controlled or oversized characteristics.

Do you support project cargo and heavy equipment?

Project-cargo coordination can be scoped to include route and site constraints, permits, lifting interfaces, border and terminal capability, convoy or escort needs and phased delivery planning with specialist providers.
