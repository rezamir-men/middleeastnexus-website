Iraq's $17 Billion Development Road: The Trade Corridor That Will Reshape the Middle East

April 2025 · Reza Mir, Founder

Iraq's Development Road is no longer a vision — it is an active construction project that will define the country's economic trajectory for the next generation. Stretching 1,200 kilometres from the Al-Faw Grand Port in Basra to the Faysh Khabur border crossing with Turkey, this dual railway and motorway corridor passes through 11 Iraqi provinces, connecting the Arabian Gulf to Europe via Turkey.

The Numbers

Total investment stands at $17 billion, with the World Bank approving $930 million in financing in June 2025 for railway modernisation alone. The project is expected to generate $4 billion annually once operational and create at least 100,000 jobs across the corridor.

What It Means for Materials Supply

A project of this scale requires staggering volumes of cement, steel, aggregates and pre-fabricated components. Middle East Nexus, with its established supply networks and customs clearance expertise built over two decades of Iraq operations, is ideally placed to support contractors working along this corridor.

Timeline

Construction is planned in three phases, targeting completion in 2028, 2033 and 2050. The southern port and first corridor segment are the immediate priority, with multiple international contractors already mobilised on site.

For investors, logistics operators and construction material suppliers, the Development Road represents one of the defining infrastructure opportunities of the decade.

الأرقام

يبلغ إجمالي الاستثمار 17 مليار دولار، مع موافقة البنك الدولي على تمويل 930 مليون دولار في يونيو 2025 لتحديث السكك الحديدية وحده. ومن المتوقع أن يولّد المشروع 4 مليارات دولار سنوياً عند التشغيل، ويخلق ما لا يقل عن 100,000 فرصة عمل.
