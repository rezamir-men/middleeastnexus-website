Babylon

A central Iraqi market combining industry, agriculture, housing and world heritage.

Regional Market Profile

Hillah & Babylon

Babylon Governorate benefits from its location south of Baghdad, strong agricultural base, industrial activity and exceptional heritage assets centred on ancient Babylon. Priority commercial themes include food processing, logistics, housing, manufacturing, water infrastructure and heritage-led tourism.
