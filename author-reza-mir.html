Reza Mir

Founder & Managing Director — Middle East Nexus Ltd

Reza Mir is the founder of Middle East Nexus, a London-based consultancy focused on Iraq market entry, infrastructure and trade. With over 20 years of on-the-ground experience since 2003, he has operated across market intelligence, infrastructure project management and international commodity trading.

He writes on Iraq's economy, politics and infrastructure, and shares a personal memoir series, From Tehran to Baghdad, drawn from two decades of building businesses across the country.
