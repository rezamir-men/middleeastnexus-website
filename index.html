Our Story

Two Decades of
Iraqi Expertise

Middle East Nexus LTD is a UK-registered consultancy founded by a pioneer with over 20 years of direct, hands-on experience in Iraq's most competitive markets.

Starting from a single retail shop in 2003, our founder built one of Iraq's largest industrial kitchens and established strategic supply partnerships with Kayson and Bolandpayeh — supplying 1,000 tonnes of cement daily to major water treatment projects in Kufah and Nasiriyah.

Today, Middle East Nexus supports Iraq's infrastructure development through UK governance, deep local knowledge and cooperation with established international engineering, investment and infrastructure partners.

Key Projects

Iraq Infrastructure
We Supplied

From 2003 onwards, Middle East Nexus (under its predecessor operations) played a direct role in supplying construction materials to some of Iraq's most critical infrastructure projects — delivering over 1,000 tonnes of cement daily at peak operations.

Water Infrastructure

Nasiriyah Water Treatment Plant

The largest water treatment project ever undertaken in Iraq — a $277 million facility producing 10,000 m³/hour of potable water, supplying five cities across Dhi Qar province and serving 1.47 million people daily. Built with 118 km of pipeline and 48,000 m³ of concrete. Middle East Nexus supplied cement and construction materials directly to this landmark project.

Water Infrastructure

Al-Kifil (Kufa) Water Treatment Plant

A major EPC turnkey project by Kayson Inc. with a capacity of 4,000 m³/hour supplying clean water to 560,000 residents of Babylon Province. The project included 56 km of transmission pipeline and 19,500 m³ of concrete works. Our team supplied 1,000+ tonnes of cement daily to this project during peak construction.

Sports Infrastructure

Tajiat Olympic Stadium, Baghdad

Iraq's largest sports infrastructure project — a 60,000-seat Olympic stadium complex in Taji, north Baghdad, contracted to Boland Payeh Co. with an investment of 227.53 billion Iraqi dinars. Includes a 4-star hotel and training facilities. Middle East Nexus was a key cement supplier during construction phases.

Transport Infrastructure

Iraq Southern Railway — Baghdad to Basra

The 550 km IRR Southern Line connecting Baghdad to Basra — Iraq's only fully operational railway — underwent major reconstruction after post-2003 damage. Passing through Karbala, Nasiriyah and Samawah, it is the backbone of Iraq's freight network. Middle East Nexus supplied construction materials to rehabilitation works along this strategic corridor.

FAQ

Questions International
Clients Ask First

How do you support a company entering Iraq?

We begin with the commercial objective, then map the market, relevant stakeholders, suitable counterparties and practical operating requirements.

Do you guarantee government approvals or contracts?

No. We do not guarantee approvals, awards or investment returns. We provide commercial support, intelligence, coordination and access based on lawful and transparent engagement.

Can you screen Iraqi partners or opportunities?

We can conduct practical commercial screening and identify early warning signs, but legal, financial, sanctions and technical due diligence should also be completed by qualified advisers.

Which clients are the best fit?

International investors, EPC contractors, manufacturers, suppliers, developers and professional firms with a serious Iraq objective.
